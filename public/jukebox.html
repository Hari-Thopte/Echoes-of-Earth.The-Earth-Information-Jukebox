Methodology & Keyboard Controls

NASA Space Apps Challenge: Developed for The Earth Information Jukebox challenge (NASA Earth Information Center / EIC), sonifying visual planetary observations into interactive soundscapes.

Multi-Domain Satellite Telemetry (2003–2025): Switch live between six planetary Earth Jukeboxes powered by NASA GIBS layers — Arctic & Antarctic Sea Ice (NSIDC Sea Ice Index & AMSR-E/AMSR2), Surface Temperature (NASA GISTEMP v4), Air Aerosols (Reference AOD), Greenness (Reference NDVI), and Rainfall (Reference mm/d).

3D Subsurface & Atmospheric Shaders: Combines satellite data grids with cryosphere parallax ice floes, coastal land/ocean masking, volumetric dust/storm layers, and live 2003 split-curtain comparison.

Shortcuts: Space (Play/Pause), Left/Right (Year), M (Mute), W (Story), R (Radar Sweep), C (Compare), H (Audio Guide), I (Radar Toggle), S (Sound Preset), G (Debug), Esc (Close).
